About The Project

About Smart Mandi

Smart Mandi helps farmers, traders, and buyers quickly discover mandi locations and track price data in a simple, streamlined dashboard environment.

Nearby mandi discovery with map support
User accounts for registration and profile management
Feedback and query collection directly inside the platform
Mission

Our mission is to make agricultural market information easier to access, easier to understand, and more useful in day-to-day decisions.

Vision

Our vision is a modern mandi discovery platform with transparent data, easy communication, and a smooth user experience for every visitor.
